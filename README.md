In this technical showcase, I focused on the design and assembly of a complex, multi-body sheet metal tray, demonstrating proficiency in managing independent sheet metal folders within a single CAD environment. I successfully implemented advanced mirroring techniques, transitioning from individual feature mirroring to Body Mirroring to ensure the structural and parametric integrity of symmetrical components. The project highlights my attention to manufacturing detail, specifically in creating tight-tolerance Closed Corners and implementing appropriate Bend Reliefs to prevent material failure during the press brake process. By utilizing Imperial (IPS) units and validating designs through Flat Pattern generation, I ensured that the multi-body assembly is 100% production-ready for precision laser cutting and fabrication.
